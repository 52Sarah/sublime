# sublime color scheme notes

Most of the good or better scheme are configured as Schemr favorites in `SchemrFavorites.sublime-settings`.

_ _ _
## dark schemes

### Tomorrow Night
	- "Packages/Theme - Phoenix/Color Scheme/Tomorrow-Night.tmTheme"
	- md: v good, moderate color
	- sh: v good, more muted than Phoenix Dark X
	- GITP

### Twilight
	- "Packages/Color Scheme - Legacy/Twilight.tmTheme"
	- md: good, lots of color
	- sh: v good, v similar to Tomorrow Night
	- DOTFILES

### Space Cadet
	- "Packages/Color Scheme - Legacy/SpaceCadet.tmTheme"
	- md: good, mainly 2 colors (ivory with blue headings)
	- sh: good, fairly muted

### Monokai Bright
	- "Packages/Color Scheme - Legacy/Monokai Bright.tmTheme"
	- md: ok, some color
	- sh: v good, muted
